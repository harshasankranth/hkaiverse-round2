- The full Academic Regulations were not found; all REDUCE and ADD changes are conditional.
- Five postings from one board, read as excerpts, mostly for experienced engineers, are a small skewed sample.
- No faculty or graduate feedback was obtained.
- The 20 to 25% share depends on my own stated definition.
